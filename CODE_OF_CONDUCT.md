# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive; critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask before concluding.
- Accept responsibility, apologise when needed, and learn.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private
information, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may
ban contributors for repeated or severe violations. Report concerns privately to the maintainers
(see `SECURITY.md` for the contact route). Reports are handled confidentially.

This document is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
